Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 2026

Quality Engineering Tools is an independent engineering-tools website operated by Rolando Fernández. This notice explains how data is collected, used, and protected when you visit this website or use its features.

Summary of data collection, processing purposes, hosting, retention, and your rights.

1. Information Collection and Purposes

This site collects information through the following features:

Demo Access form: When you request temporary 48-hour access to tools, the form collects your name, work email address, tool selection, and optionally your company name and message. This data is used solely to evaluate and process your request and to send your demo access details.

Feedback form: When you submit feedback, the form collects your selected tool, feedback category (bug, suggestion, missing feature, general feedback), message, and an optional email address. This data is used solely to evaluate feedback and improve the tools. The optional email is used only if follow-up clarification is helpful.

Selected-link click tracking: Interactions with certain high-value links (such as call-to-action buttons or outbound profile links) trigger a lightweight submission via Netlify Forms recording an event identifier, the page path, and the referrer. This is used to understand high-level navigation trends without tracking individual user identities.

Cloudflare Web Analytics: The site loads Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate site performance and visitor trends (such as page views and visitor counts). This analytics tool is privacy-oriented: it does not use cookies, local storage, or persistent cross-site tracking to profile individual visitors.

2. Hosting and Infrastructure

This website is hosted on Netlify, which delivers static assets and processes form submissions. When you browse the site or submit a form, Netlify’s servers may process technical metadata associated with your connection (such as your IP address, browser user-agent header, and request timestamps). This metadata is processed to securely serve web content, mitigate automated abuse or spam (including via honeypot fields), and ensure system reliability.

3. Data Retention

Data is retained conservatively: information collected through form submissions is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose (such as evaluating demo requests, addressing feedback, or maintaining service reliability), unless a longer period is legally required.

4. Cookies and Consent

The current site does not use a cookie consent banner because the current analytics configuration does not use client-side tracking cookies. If the data-processing setup or analytics configuration changes, this notice will be updated accordingly.

5. Data Protection Rights and Contact

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation or similar data protection frameworks), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, or to object to its processing.

Because no dedicated public contact email is established in this repository, you may exercise these rights or make privacy-related inquiries by submitting a message via the Feedback form (selecting "General feedback") or by contacting Rolando Fernández through his public GitHub profile.
