/01 · OVERVIEW

SPC is a decision, not just a number

A calculated index is only one part of the answer. The useful question is what the data say about the process, whether the process is behaving predictably, and whether the result is strong enough for the decision being made.

The guide brings those questions into one workflow. Instead of showing formulas without context, it connects the data, the chart, the stability decision and the capability result so the reasoning can be followed.

It is built as a working companion for people who need to apply SPC in a quality or manufacturing context: choose the appropriate study, read the signals, calculate the relevant indices and make a defensible next decision.

Working principle. The guide explains and operationalises the reference material. It does not replace the licensed AIAG-VDA manual, applicable customer requirements, or the professional review of the process context.

/02 · WHAT IT HELPS YOU DO

Move from data to a defensible decision

The guide supports the sequence a quality engineer actually needs: understand the data, establish statistical control, assess capability and communicate the result with its limits intact.

  • Statistical Process ControlFollow a practical SPC workflow instead of treating control-chart analysis as an isolated calculation.
  • Process stabilityDistinguish predictable process behaviour from a process that still contains special causes requiring action.
  • Control chart interpretationRead location and variation together, understand the signal and avoid reacting to common-cause variation.
  • Process capabilityConnect the process spread and centring to the applicable index without losing the assumptions behind it.
  • Statistical guidanceUse formulas, constants and reference points as support for the decision—not as a replacement for engineering reasoning.
  • Interpretation of resultsSeparate what the result means statistically from what it means for the process, product and next action.
  • Practical decision supportKnow when to hold, investigate, improve or request more evidence before moving to a conclusion.

/03 · HOW IT WORKS

A simple sequence, kept visible

The guide keeps the sequence explicit so a calculation never gets separated from the question it is meant to answer.

  1. /01

    Bring the data

    Start with the measured process data and the study context that gives it meaning.

  2. /02

    Inspect the chart and determine whether the process is behaving predictably.

  3. /03

  4. /04

    Make the decision

    Turn the evidence into the next engineering action and record what still needs review.
