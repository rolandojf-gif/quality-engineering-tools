/01 · OVERVIEW

From audit preparation to a usable finding

An audit is not only a sequence of questions. It is a chain of context: what was planned, what was observed, what evidence supports the observation, and what has to happen next.

The copilot is designed to make that chain easier to follow. Instead of treating preparation, execution, assessment and follow-up as disconnected activities, it gives the audit team a common place to work through the process and keep the result connected to the question that produced it.

It is a support workspace for professional judgement—not a substitute for the VDA 6.3 text, customer-specific requirements or the decision of the auditor. The useful part is the discipline: make the basis of an assessment visible before moving to the next step.

Working principle. The tool should make the audit conversation more consistent and reviewable. It does not create a finding without context, and it does not turn an assessment into a claim that the underlying standard has been satisfied.

/02 · WHAT IT HELPS YOU DO

The work around the question

Use the tool to keep the audit practical and reviewable: know what to prepare, work through the conversation deliberately, and leave with an assessment that others can follow.

  • Audit preparationFrame the scope, gather the relevant process context and enter the audit with a clearer starting point.
  • Process audit executionKeep the conversation connected to the process question instead of reducing the audit to an isolated checklist.
  • Evaluation of VDA 6.3 questionsWork through each question as a defined assessment point, with the basis for the response kept in view.
  • Evidence and findingsConnect what was observed to the question that prompted it, so the result can be reviewed later.
  • Risk identificationMake potential risk visible as part of the audit record rather than leaving it in unstructured notes.
  • Audit scoringMake the assessment explicit and reviewable; the copilot supports consistency without inventing a score or replacing professional judgement.
  • Structured documentationTurn a sequence of conversations into a coherent record that can be shared and challenged.
  • Follow-up / reviewCarry findings into a defined review loop so the audit produces actions, not just observations.

/03 · HOW IT WORKS

A deliberate path through the audit

The workflow is intentionally simple: establish context, work through the assessment, and make the result reviewable before closing the loop.

  1. /01

    Set the scope

    Define the process and audit context so the team starts from a shared reference.

  2. /02

    Prepare the evidence

    Collect the context needed to ask informed questions and understand the process.

  3. /03

    Conduct the review

    Move through the VDA 6.3 questions and capture the evidence behind each assessment.

  4. /04

    Close the loop

    Review findings, make the follow-up visible and return to the record with a clear next step.
